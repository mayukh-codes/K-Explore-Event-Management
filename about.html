About EventSphere

Connecting people to extraordinary experiences since 2023

Our Mission

Making Every Moment Memorable

EventSphere was born from a simple belief: great experiences bring people together. We built a platform that makes discovering, planning, and attending events effortless — whether you're looking for your next tech conference, a wellness retreat, or an electrifying music festival.

We partner with event organizers across India to bring their events to a wider audience, providing them with powerful tools to manage registrations and connect with passionate attendees.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

January 2023

The Idea

Arjun and Priya met at a poorly organized tech conference and thought: "We can do this better."

June 2023

EventSphere Launches

First version goes live with 10 events listed. 200 registrations in the first month.

2024

Rapid Growth

Expanded to 30 cities, onboarded 100+ partner organisations, hit 10,000 registered users.

2025

Series A Funding

Raised ₹8 Crore to expand the team and build a mobile app. Reached 120+ cities.

2026

50,000 Attendees & Counting

Today, EventSphere is India's fastest-growing event discovery platform. And we're just getting started.
